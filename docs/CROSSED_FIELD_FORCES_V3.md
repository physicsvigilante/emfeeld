# Static electric and magnetic force observations

Run the [velocity selector](../examples/magnetic/velocity_selector.json) using `python -m emfeeld magnetic-field examples/magnetic/velocity_selector.json`. These illustrative vacuum fields are prescribed and static. Select different source arrangements and velocities in the model data.

Version 3 uses kind `static_electromagnetic_field`. It keeps the V2 magnetic `sources`, `probes` and `force_observations` arrays and adds a nonempty `electric_sources` array. Each electric record has a unique `id` and the existing electric scene source parameters for its type. Electric and magnetic source IDs share one namespace. A probe position is in world metres. Every force request uses that same named position for both fields, its own signed charge in coulombs and its supplied laboratory velocity in m/s. Speed must be below c. V1 and V2 retain their existing contracts.

The report stores magnetic probes in `probes` (tesla) and electric probes in `electric_probes` (N/C), indexed by the same probe IDs. Each force row has `electric.force_N`, `magnetic.force_N` and `force_N`, all in newtons. They represent qE, q(v × B) and their signed vector sum. Multiplication and addition use exact fractions of the reported field, charge and velocity components before final binary64 conversion. An unavailable force contribution gives a null total and preserves the available contribution. A successfully computed zero remains a three-component vector. Global status counts electric probes, magnetic probes and force requests. Exit codes and fresh-output protections follow V2. The input hash and the runtime values of ε₀ and μ₀ accompany the report.

In the example E is 2 N/C along +y and B is 0.5 T along +z. A positive 1 µC charge moving along +x at 4 m/s has electric force +2 µN and magnetic force −2 µN along y. At 2 m/s the net force is +1 µN and at 6 m/s it is −1 µN. Reversing the charge reverses each component, preserving balance. Adding velocity along B preserves magnetic force. This is an instantaneous force observation; transmission through a finite apparatus would require a trajectory and geometry.

Electric fields use the numerical domains and physical assumptions of the corresponding [analytic sources](PHYSICS.md). Wire fields use the [static magnetic field contract](STATIC_MAGNETIC_FIELD_V1.md). No electric or magnetic potentials enter the force sum.
